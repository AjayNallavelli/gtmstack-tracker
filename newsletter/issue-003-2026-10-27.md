## 2 pricing things worth knowing

**1. Snov.io vs Prospeo: same $99, different seat math.** On monthly billing, Snov.io Pro S and Prospeo Growth both cost $99 for 5,000 credits a month. The difference is seats: Snov.io includes unlimited seats on every plan, while Prospeo's credits are per user. At the entry level, Snov.io Starter is $39 for 1,000 credits and Prospeo Starter is $49 for 2,000, so Prospeo is cheaper per credit if one person does the prospecting. Prospeo's free tier is also bigger (100 credits a month vs Snov.io's 50-credit trial), and signing up through our link adds 20% free credits on your first purchase. [Snov.io vs Prospeo](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/snovio-vs-prospeo/) · [Try Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/?via=ajay) *(affiliate)*

**2. Saleshandy vs Klenty: cheapest entry vs unlimited users.** Saleshandy Starter is $34/mo on annual billing ($41 monthly), and Pro is $76/mo annual ($99 monthly). Klenty Starter is $50/mo billed annually, but it covers unlimited users and mailboxes, priced by contacts instead of seats. For a solo founder, Saleshandy is cheaper. For an SDR team or agency sharing one plan, Klenty Starter can come out ahead. Klenty only shows annual or quarterly prices, and has a 14-day free trial.
