## 2 pricing things worth knowing

**1. Hunter vs Prospeo: same $49 start, different scaling.** Both Starter plans are $49/mo for 2,000 credits on monthly billing. On annual billing Hunter is slightly cheaper ($34/mo vs $37/mo). At higher volume, Hunter Growth gives 10,000 credits for $149 (about 1.5 cents each), while Prospeo Growth gives 5,000 for $99 (about 2 cents each). Prospeo has the bigger free tier (100 credits a month vs Hunter's 50), but its credits are per user. Signing up through our Prospeo link adds 20% free credits on your first purchase. [Hunter vs Prospeo](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/hunter-vs-prospeo/) · [Try Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/?via=ajay) *(affiliate)*

**2. Instantly vs Smartlead: the entry plan is a contacts question.** Smartlead Base is $39/mo for 6,000 emails and 2,000 contacts. Instantly Outreach Growth is $47/mo for 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts, so Smartlead is cheaper to start. One step up flips it: Instantly Hypergrowth is $97/mo for 125,000 emails and 25,000 contacts, against Smartlead Pro at $94/mo for 90,000 emails and 30,000 contacts. High-volume senders get more emails per dollar on Instantly, and list-heavy teams get more contacts on Smartlead. [Instantly vs Smartlead](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/instantly-vs-smartlead/)
