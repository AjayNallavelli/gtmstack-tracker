3 pricing things worth knowing

1. Hunter and Prospeo cost the same monthly, but not yearly. Both charge $49/mo for 2,000 credits on monthly billing. On annual billing Hunter is $34/mo (30% off) and Prospeo is $37/mo (25% off). Prospeo's free tier is bigger (100 credits a month vs Hunter's 50), and signing up through our link gets 20% extra credits on your first purchase. Hunter vs Prospeo · Try Prospeo (affiliate)

2. At small volume, flat plans beat pay-per-prospect. Woodpecker charges $7 per 100 contacted prospects ($70 for 1,000 on monthly billing, 25% less on annual), with unlimited team members and email accounts. Instantly Outreach Growth is $47/mo for 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails, and Smartlead Base is $39/mo for 2,000 contacts. Woodpecker makes more sense for agencies and teams whose send volume swings month to month, or who need lots of seats. Instantly vs Woodpecker · Try Woodpecker (affiliate)

3. Klenty doesn't price by seat on its entry plan. Starter is $50/mo billed annually with unlimited users and mailboxes (priced by contacts, shown at 15,000 contacts and 75,000 emails a month). Growth ($70) and Plus ($99) are per user. It only shows annual or quarterly prices, but there's a 14-day free trial. Instantly vs Klenty · lemlist vs Klenty · Try Klenty (affiliate)
