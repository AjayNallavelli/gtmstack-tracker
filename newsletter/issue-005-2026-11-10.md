# What changed in AI sales tool pricing: week of Nov 10

We check the pricing pages of **39 AI SDR, cold email, enrichment and deliverability tools** every day. Three tools made real pricing moves this week.

## Price and plan changes

**Instantly: Outreach Hypergrowth went up (Nov 3).**
- Old: $97/mo monthly, $77.60/mo billed yearly
- New: **$107/mo** monthly, **$85.58/mo** billed yearly
- Still 125,000 emails and 25,000 uploaded contacts. Growth ($47) and Light Speed ($358) are unchanged.
[Instantly on GTMStack](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/tools/instantly/)

**La Growth Machine: new per-identity plans (Nov 4).**
- Now: Basic €60/mo, Pro €120/mo, Ultimate €180/mo per sending identity on monthly billing. Annual billing is €50, €100 and €150.
- Team members join free, so you pay only for each identity that sends.
- Prices are published in euros only.
[La Growth Machine on GTMStack](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/tools/lagrowthmachine/)

**Findymail: usage-based headline plans, cheaper credit entry (Nov 10).**
- New headline plans: Individual **$99**, Team **$399**, Scale **$999** per month, with usage instead of credit counting.
- Credit plans moved behind a "See credit-based plans" link and now start at **Basic $49/mo for 1,000 credits**. The entry credit plan was previously Starter at $99 for 5,000 credits.
[Findymail on GTMStack](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/tools/findymail/)

Wording-only edits (not price changes): ZoomInfo, Prospeo, Snov.io, People Data Labs and Allegrow. Full log: [What changed](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/changes/)

## 2 things worth knowing

**1. Instantly's bundle is now the better deal at the middle.** With Hypergrowth at $107 for 125,000 emails, Smartlead Pro at $94 for 90,000 emails and 30,000 contacts is cheaper by $13, though Instantly sends about 40% more email. If you send under about 90,000 a month, Smartlead is the cheaper path. [Instantly vs Smartlead](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/instantly-vs-smartlead/)

**2. Enrichment entry prices are converging near $50.** Findymail Basic is $49 for 1,000 credits. FullEnrich Pro is about $55 for 1,000 credits ($0.055 each) and you only pay for found data. Findymail's usage plans start at $99 with no per-credit math.
