## 2 pricing things worth knowing

**1. Prospeo vs FullEnrich: cost per credit.** Prospeo Starter is $49/mo for 2,000 credits (about 2.5 cents each); Prospeo Pro is $249/mo for 15,000 (about 1.7 cents). FullEnrich Pro is $55/mo for 1,000 credits (5.5 cents) and its 15,000-credit plan is $720 (4.8 cents). FullEnrich runs a waterfall across 15+ providers and charges only for data it finds, so cost per found email can differ from cost per credit. Prospeo credits are per user; FullEnrich allows unlimited users. [Prospeo vs FullEnrich](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/prospeo-vs-fullenrich/) · [Try FullEnrich](https://try.fullenrich.com/5k4aeo7b0ocx) *(affiliate)* · [Try Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/?via=ajay) *(affiliate)*

**2. Instantly vs Woodpecker: flat plan vs per-prospect billing.** Instantly Outreach Growth is a flat $47/mo ($37.60 billed yearly) for 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts. Woodpecker bills usage: $7 per 100 contacted prospects, so 1,000 prospects a month is about $70, and 3,000 is about $210. Woodpecker has unlimited team members and email accounts, and adds LinkedIn at $29/mo per account. Small, steady lists favor Instantly's flat price. Spiky volume favors paying per prospect.
