# GTMStack Tracker #1: the baseline

Hi! This is the first issue of GTMStack Tracker. We now take a snapshot of the pricing pages of **39 AI SDR, cold email, data/enrichment and deliverability tools** every day. When a price, plan or limit changes, it goes into the [What changed](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/changes/) log and into this newsletter.

Nothing has "changed" yet, because today is day one. So this issue covers the starting point: what the pages said on **October 19, 2026**.

> Prices are in USD unless noted, taken from each vendor's public pricing page on 2026-10-19. "Annual" means the per-month price when billed yearly.

## The headline numbers

- **39 tools tracked.** 27 publish full pricing, 5 publish part of it, and **7 hide it behind "contact sales"**: 11x, Artisan, Outreach, Salesloft, ZoomInfo, Cognism and Seamless.AI.
- **16 tools have a $0 tier**, including Apollo, Clay, Hunter, Prospeo, Regie.ai, Lusha and Kaspr.
- **Annual billing usually saves 17–30%.** Hunter is at the top of that range (30%, so Starter is $49 monthly or $34 on annual). Prospeo is 25%, and Instantly and lemlist are about 20%.

## AI SDR agents: the best-known ones don't publish prices

- **11x** and **Artisan**, probably the two most-discussed AI SDRs, don't list any prices. 11x.ai/pricing returns a 404, and Artisan says pricing is "scoped on your plan."
- **AiSDR** charges by usage: **$1.50 per person prospected**, billed daily. It also sells a fully managed service at $2,500/mo.
- **Reply.io's AI SDR** starts at **$800/mo** on monthly billing ($500/mo on annual) for 1,000 active contacts. The Growth tier starts at $2,500/mo ($1,500 on annual).
- **Amplemarket Startup** costs **$600/mo** on an annual term and includes 2 users and 27,000 contacts. Its Growth and Elite tiers are custom-priced.
- **Regie.ai** has the lowest paid entry in the category: **Pro at $49/mo** ($41 on annual) with 5,000 credits a month. There is also a free tier with 250 one-time credits.

## Cold email: entry plans cost $29–$69, and the top of the market sits near $360–$380 for 500k sends

| Tool | Entry plan (monthly billing) | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Mailshake | Starter **$29** ($25 on annual) | entry outreach plan |
| Smartlead | Base **$39** | 6,000 emails/mo, 2,000 contacts |
| Snov.io | Starter **$39** | 1,000 credits/mo, unlimited seats |
| Saleshandy | Starter **$41** ($34 on annual) | — |
| Instantly | Outreach Growth **$47** | 5,000 emails/mo, 1,000 contacts, unlimited accounts + warmup |
| lemlist | Email **$69** ($55 on annual) | 50,000 emails/mo, unlimited users |

- **At 500,000 emails a month,** Instantly Light Speed costs **$358/mo** and Smartlead Unlimited Prime costs **$379/mo**.
- **Woodpecker** charges by usage: **$7 per 100 contacted prospects**, with team members and email accounts unlimited.
- **lemwarm is no longer sold on its own** on lemlist.com. Warmup now comes bundled with lemlist plans.

## Data & enrichment: from $0.055 per credit to $6K-a-year contracts

- **Clay** prices actions and data credits separately. **Launch is $185/mo** on monthly billing ($167/mo annual) for 15k actions and 2.5k data credits a month. **Growth is $495/mo** ($446 annual).
- **Apollo** has a free plan with 900 credits a year. Paid seats on annual billing cost **$49 (Basic), $79 (Professional) and $119 (Organization, 3-seat minimum)** per month.
- **Hunter Starter** and **Prospeo Starter** cost the same: **$49/mo for 2,000 credits**.
- **FullEnrich** charges **$0.055 per credit** at 1,000 credits a month (about $55/mo). Credits are only spent when data is found.
- **Findymail** starts at **$99/mo**. **BetterContact** starts at **$15/mo** for 200 credits.
- **ZoomInfo** and **Cognism** are quote-only. **RocketReach** individual plans cost **$33–$142/mo** billed annually, and its team plans "start at $6K annually."
- **Clearbit is now HubSpot Breeze Intelligence.** It's sold through Data Hub plus HubSpot Credits rather than as a standalone price.

## Deliverability & warmup: $15–$99 to start

- **Warmbox Solo** costs **$19/mo** ($15 on annual) for 1 inbox. **MailReach** costs **$25 per mailbox per month** ($20 on annual) and includes spam tests.
- **EmailGuard Pro** costs **$49/mo** for 25 domains, 100 inboxes and 300 placement tests. **GlockApps Essential** costs **$59/mo** billed annually.
- **Allegrow Starter** costs **$99/mo** for 5,000 verifications a month, and Scale Plus Unlimited is **$1,340/mo** on annual billing.

## What we'll watch next

Credit-based plans (Clay, Apollo, Instantly credits, Prospeo) are where limits tend to change quietly. Our daily diff flags new or removed price points and changed plan wording, so you'll see it here first.
