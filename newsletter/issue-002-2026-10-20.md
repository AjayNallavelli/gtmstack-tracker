# What changed in AI sales tool pricing: week of Oct 20

We check the pricing pages of **39 AI SDR, cold email, enrichment and deliverability tools** every day. Tracking started on Oct 19, so this week covers two checks.

## This week: no price changes

- **No tool changed a price, plan or limit.** Every published price point is the same as on Oct 19.
- **ZoomInfo rewrote its pricing page.** The old taglines are gone, replaced by short product-line blurbs (prospecting, ABM, inbound, data, renewals, talent) and a new line: *"Pay once. Re-pull it free for a year."* There are still no published prices, and every option is "free trial" or "talk to sales." ## 3 pricing things worth knowing

**1. Hunter and Prospeo cost the same monthly, but not yearly.** Both charge $49/mo for 2,000 credits on monthly billing. On annual billing Hunter is $34/mo (30% off) and Prospeo is $37/mo (25% off). Prospeo's free tier is bigger (100 credits a month vs Hunter's 50), and signing up through our link gets 20% extra credits on your first purchase. [Hunter vs Prospeo](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/hunter-vs-prospeo/) · [Try Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/?via=ajay) *(affiliate)*

**2. At small volume, flat plans beat pay-per-prospect.** Woodpecker charges $7 per 100 contacted prospects ($70 for 1,000 on monthly billing, 25% less on annual), with unlimited team members and email accounts. Instantly Outreach Growth is $47/mo for 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails, and Smartlead Base is $39/mo for 2,000 contacts. Woodpecker makes more sense for agencies and teams whose send volume swings month to month, or who need lots of seats. [Instantly vs Woodpecker](https://ajaynallavelli.github.io/gtmstack-tracker/compare/instantly-vs-woodpecker/) · [Try Woodpecker](https://woodpecker.co/?red=gtmsta) *(affiliate)*

**3. Klenty doesn't price by seat on its entry plan.** Starter is $50/mo billed annually with unlimited users and mailboxes (priced by contacts, shown at 15,000 contacts and 75,000 emails a month). Growth ($70) and Plus ($99) are per user. It only shows annual or quarterly prices, but there's a 14-day free trial.
