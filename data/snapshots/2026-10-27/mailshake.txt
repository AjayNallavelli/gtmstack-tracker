Frequently Asked Questions
How am I charged?
Billing is paid by your team and is based on how many users you purchase.
How many mail accounts can I send from?
With each user you pay for, your team may connect 1 mail account. So you might choose to pay for 3 users but only have 1 person on your team so that you can connect 3 mail accounts.
What mail accounts does Mailshake work with?
Mailshake works with Gmail, G Suite, Office 365, most Outlook and Exchange accounts, and most any email address that supports SMTP and IMAP connections.
How am I charged if I change my subscription in the middle of my billing cycle?
Your costs will be prorated, so you will either have a pending charge or credit on your account.

Is there a free trial?
No, we collect payment up front, however, we offer concierge onboarding, deliverability training, and support resources to help you be successful.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, as long as you cancel your plan before its renewal date you won‘t be charged again (unless you have outstanding charges such as pending prorated costs from changing your subscription).
Does Mailshake work for agencies?
Yes! We recommend having your client create their own team so they are the billed party and add you as a user on their team. You can belong to as many teams as you like and it‘s easy to switch between teams. Unless you have your own team, you will not be billed.
