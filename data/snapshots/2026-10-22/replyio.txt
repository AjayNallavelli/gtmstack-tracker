Frequently Asked Questions
What is Reply.io’s sales engagement platform & cold email outreach software?
Reply.io’s cold email outreach software and sales engagement platform helps businesses, founders, solopreneurs, marketers, sales, and SDR teams scale their outreach efforts seamlessly. It includes unlimited email inboxes, email warmup functionality, unlimited users and contact storage, and multi-channel automation to help get more replies and book more meetings. Additionally, Reply.io offers new feature – AI SDR, ensuring your outreach is always efficient and effective.
Do you really offer “Unlimited Mailboxes and Warmups”?
The Fair Usage Policy applies to Email Volume plans and ensures that the number of mailboxes included in your subscription aligns with your active contacts and sending activity.
This approach helps ensure you have enough mailboxes to reach your contacts effectively, while also following email deliverability best practices and maintaining stable platform performance.
The number of included mailboxes is determined based on your active contact limit and overall usage. Learn more
here
..
What is “Active Contacts”?
Active contacts is the number of unique contacts you can send 1 first-step email and an unlimited number of follow-ups per month.
What is “Unlimited Emails Monthly”?
Some other cold outreach tools limit the number of emails and follow-ups you can send per month. For example, you might be limited to sending 5,000 emails to 1,000 active contacts. With Reply, you can send as many emails to active contacts as you need
What is “Unlimited contact storage”?
The default prospects limit in the Reply account is 100,000 on all Business and Agency plans. If you cannot upload more contacts, our Support team can raise the limit depending on your current plan. Learn more
here
.
Can I integrate Reply.io with CRMs & other tools I’m using?
Absolutely, you can integrate Reply directly with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Copper, and Close. This allows you to easily sync data such as contacts, accounts, calls, tasks, statuses, and activities.
