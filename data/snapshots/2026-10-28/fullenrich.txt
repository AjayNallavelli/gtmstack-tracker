Frequently
Asked
Questions.
What is "waterfall enrichment"?
Waterfall enrichment is a process in which you use several different data sources, one after the other, to find a person's email and phone number. The more sources you use, the more contact information you can find, and the more potential customers you can reach.
What is your email & phone find rate?
Our email and phone find rate depends on industry, geography, and other factors. As an estimate, it averages at around 80%, but it can be considerably higher. Try it for yourself with your free credits.
How do credits work?
Credits are used when we successfully find verified data. 1 work email found = credits used. 1 phone number found = credits used. If we can't find the data, you don't pay. Credits roll over for 3 months on monthly plans and 12 months on yearly plans.
What if I only need emails, or only phone numbers?
The credits in your plan can be used to find phone numbers, emails, or both. Once your plan is purchased, you can select which type of enrichment you want.
Can I add unlimited users?
Yes! Every plan — including the free trial — comes with unlimited users at no additional cost. Add your entire team without worrying about per-seat pricing.
Are you GDPR & CCPA Compliant?
Yes. We do not have any contact database, meaning we do not store contact information. We only select providers that are CCPA and GDPR compliant. We are also SOC 2 Type II certified.
