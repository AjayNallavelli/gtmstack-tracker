Frequently Asked Questions
What does the 14-day free trial include?
You’ll get access to the Multichannel plan’s features (except in-app calls), plus 200 free credits to find emails and phone numbers, explore buying signals, and more. You can also try lemAgent with your own data and see what it creates before upgrading to launch it.
Which plan should I choose based on my size and volume?
It depends on the channels you use and how much you reach out. The email plan lets you choose your monthly email volume and send safely at scale, with unlimited users, senders, and campaigns. With Multichannel reach, you can reach leads across email, LinkedIn, and calls, with SMS and WhatsApp available as add-ons. Pricing is based on the number of users. Go for Enterprise if you need a custom plan for larger teams with specific volume, security, flexibility, or support requirements.
How do lemlist credits work?
Credits are lemlist’s currency for accessing Data features, including finding contact information, enriching leads, and receiving buying signals. You can subscribe to a monthly credit allowance and are charged only for successful results. Unused credits roll over for as long as your subscription remains active.
When should I choose the Enterprise plan?
Enterprise is a good fit for larger or fast-growing teams that need centralized control. Think IT-managed orgs requiring SSO, companies with stricter compliance needs, or finance teams that can't pay by card. If self-serve no longer cuts it and you need structure, auditing, and dedicated support, Enterprise is the right move.
Is lemwarm and deliverability protection included?
Yes. lemwarm and built-in deliverability protections are included in all plans to help keep your emails out of spam and protect your sender reputation at any scale.
