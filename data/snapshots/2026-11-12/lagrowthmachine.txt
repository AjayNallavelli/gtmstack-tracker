What is an identity, and do team members pay?
An identity is one person who sends: their LinkedIn account and their email inboxes, each with its own dedicated mobile proxy. You pay per identity. Teammates who import leads, prepare campaigns, qualify leads and answer replies join for free: 3 members on Basic, 25 on Pro, unlimited on Ultimate. Only identities launch campaigns.
Which plan do I need for AI, the MCP, the API or CRM sync?
AI messages are included in every plan, Basic too. The MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT, Reply Agent, the API and the Zapier, Make, Clay and n8n integrations start on Pro, and you can try them all during the 14-day free trial. CRM sync with HubSpot and Pipedrive and LGM Analysis Agent come with Ultimate.
Monthly or yearly: can I switch later?
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, add or remove an identity, or change your billing period whenever you want, straight from the app.
How do credits work, and what happens if I need more?
Every plan comes with monthly credits per identity: 1,250 on Basic, 2,000 on Pro and 5,000 on Ultimate. A LinkedIn profile costs 1 credit, an email 5, a phone number 20, and credits are only used when data is found. Need more? Top up anytime from your billing settings, at €0.01 per credit.
What is in the free trial?
14 days, no credit card. You test La Growth Machine with one identity and ready-to-use sequence templates. Want to build your own sequences during the trial? Ask our support in the chat. Nothing is charged at the end unless you choose a plan.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes, and they are public. The more identities you have and the longer you commit, the less you pay per identity. 1 to 2 identities: −5% quarterly, 2 months free yearly. 3 to 5 identities: −9% quarterly, −20% yearly. 6 identities and more: −15% quarterly, −30% yearly. Companies with a social or environmental mission get 50% off: ask our support in the chat.
What support do I get?
Human support from our own team: first reply within 24 business hours on Basic, within 2 business hours on Pro and Ultimate. From 3 identities on Pro and Ultimate, a dedicated Outreach Advisor works with you: a 4-month launch program, then quarterly reviews.
Invoices, VAT and currency
All your identities sit on one billing account, invoiced to your company. Prices are shown in euros or US dollars to help you compare; we always invoice in euros. Prices exclude VAT: we only add French VAT for companies based in France.
