Frequently asked questions
Your questions, answered
How do Prospeo credits work?
One credit gives you one verified business email, while a direct mobile number costs 10 credits. Your full credit pack is added at the start of every billing cycle. Credits reset on renewal and do not roll over.
Do credits roll over if I don't use them all?
No — credits do NOT roll over. Any unused credits expire at renewal, and your credit balance resets each billing cycle.
What happens if I run out of credits?
If you have no more credits, you won't be able to export or reveal new enriched contacts (emails/mobiles) until your credits reset at the start of your next billing cycle.
To get more credits right away, you can upgrade your plan or purchase a Credits Add-on: go to prospeo.io/pricing → use the slider to select a Credits Add-on → click Update Current Plan (or upgrade) → checkout.
Note: Credits are added immediately and your credit balance resets at your next billing cycle.
How can I add credits or seats for my team?
Open Account Settings → Billing.
Credits
: choose an add-on starting at 1,000 credits for $10.
Seats
: add users mid-cycle; we bill and credit pro-rated to the day. Removing seats is instant and lowers your next invoice.
Can I cancel, upgrade, or downgrade my plan whenever I want?
Absolutely. Go to Account Settings → Billing and choose the action you need.
Upgrades apply right away; you pay only the pro-rated difference.
Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current cycle, so you keep what you've already paid for.
Switching between monthly and yearly is instant.
Is there a free plan or trial, and what happens when it ends?
Yes. The Free plan provides 100 credits a month plus core filters and has no time limit. If you later upgrade and then cancel, you keep your paid features until the period ends, after which you automatically return to Free.
If I purchase a yearly plan, do I get all the credits all at once?
Yes — with a yearly plan, all included credits are granted upfront and reset at your annual renewal date. Example: If you purchase Yearly Pro plan on Jan 1, 2026, you receive 180,000 credits immediately and your credits reset on Jan 1, 2027
What is the cost if I don't want email and mobile?
Browsing and searching are free — you can explore and preview results without revealing contact details. Exports use credits: people exports always include email enrichment by default and cost 1 credit per email found (0 if no email is found). Each exported contact also includes company data, firmographics, and 30+ additional data points.
How much does company export cost?
One company record with all of the data points will cost one credit per export. This includes all available company information such as firmographics, technology stack, revenue, funding, and other data points.
Can Prospeo plug into my existing workflow?
Every plan connects to Clay, Zapier, Outreach, Lemlist, and CSV export. Growth and Pro also offer native HubSpot and Salesforce sync, so enriched contacts flow straight into your CRM and sequences without manual work.
Does Prospeo work for both small teams and large enterprises?
Yes. Solo founders can scale with the most affordable data in the industry. Growing sales teams usually pick the Growth plan. Enterprises choose Pro plus add-ons, unlocking higher rate limits, job-change tracking, native Salesforce and HubSpot sync, and the option to add millions of credits when needed.
