Pricing FAQs
How does Cognism pricing work?
Cognism pricing is tailored based on the product you need, your team setup and how you plan to use the platform.
Sales Prospecting is offered in Standard and Pro packages.
CRM Enrichment and Data-as-a-Service are priced based on subscription structure, usage and delivery requirements.
What's the difference between Standard and Pro?
Standard is designed for teams that need reliable contact and company data for day-to-day prospecting.
Pro is designed for teams that need more advanced targeting, better prioritisation and stronger visibility into prospecting performance. It includes features such as intent data, on-demand mobile verification and enhanced dashboards.
Which Sales Prospecting package is right for my team?
Standard is usually the right fit for teams that need a strong prospecting foundation and core workflow coverage.
Pro is typically a better fit for teams with more advanced targeting needs, more complex workflows or a stronger focus on efficiency and performance at scale.
How do credits work?
Cognism uses a credits model where one credit is used each time a contact record is revealed.
Once a contact has been redeemed, your team can continue using that record throughout the contract. A credit is only used again if that contact changes jobs.
Can I buy CRM Enrichment as a standalone product?
Yes. CRM Enrichment can be purchased as a standalone product or as an add-on to your existing Cognism setup.
What are verified mobile numbers?
Verified mobile numbers are phone-verified contact numbers designed to help sales teams connect with the right decision-makers more reliably. This helps improve connect rates and make outreach more efficient.
What is on-demand mobile verification?
On-demand mobile verification is a premium feature designed to enhance the accuracy and effectiveness of your outreach efforts. It allows users to select high-priority prospects and submit them for advanced verification through Cognism's verification engine. This process ensures that the contact information, particularly mobile numbers, is accurate and up-to-date, facilitating more successful connections.
What is Intent Data?
Intent data shows when companies are actively researching topics related to your solution. This helps teams prioritise accounts that may be in-market and time outreach more effectively.
Cognism uses Bombora's Company Surge
