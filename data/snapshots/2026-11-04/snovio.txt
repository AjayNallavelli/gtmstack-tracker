Frequently asked questions
What’s included in my Trial plan?
Trial is a renewable plan that lets you test Snov.io tools before you commit to a paid plan. On trial you can search for leads, verify emails, enrich data, find phone numbers, warm up your sender account, send campaigns and manage deals in the CRM. You will get 50 credits, 100 recipients, 25 enrichment tokens and 1 email warm-up slot. Premium features like integrations, bulk search, bulk verification, teamwork, email A/B testing, API & webhooks access and export are not available in Trial.
When does my Trial plan end?
It doesn't end, but renews every month. Your free credits, tokens and recipients also renew every 30 days. Please note that the Trial plan has been created to offer a peek into what Snov.io can do and is not meant to help grow sales. To see actual sales growth and get the most out of Snov.io tools, we recommend upgrading to a Sales Suite plan that actually fits your business needs.
Can I try LinkedIn Automation for free?
Yes, you can! All users can access a free 7-day trial of LinkedIn Automation.
Can I try Enrichment?
Yes. With your plan you'll receive a number of free enrichment tokens that will renew every month. The number of tokens included varies from 25 on Trial to 750 on Ultra, depending on the size of the plan.

What is a credit?
Credit is a payment unit exchanged for Email Finder, Verifier services, and certain Enrichment methods relating to email search and verification. You can distribute your credits between the tools as you like.

What is a recipient?
Recipient is a payment unit in the Campaigns tool. 1 recipient is charged when you first contact a lead. All follow-ups to that lead in the same billing period are free and unlimited.

What is an enrichment token?
Enrichment token is a payment unit exchanged for Enrichment operations. Each enrichment operation costs a varying number of tokens.

How can I spend my credits, tokens and recipients?
You can spend credits, tokens and recipients by using Snov.io services.
Credits can be used on Finder and Verifier tools, as well as certain Enrichment methods for email search and verification. Unused credits expire every 30 days, but can roll over to the next month when on annual plan or with enabled plan renewal.
Recipients are used in Campaigns. 1 recipients = 1 contacted prospect. After first contact, the follow-ups to that prospects are free and unlimited within the billing period. Unused recipients expired after 30 days and do not roll over.
