How Outreach pricing works:
Outreach pricing combines two models: seat-based access, where you pay for each user, and consumption-based AI credits, where you pay for what you use. AI credits power your agentic AI workflows—research, personalization, sequencing automation, conversation analysis, forecasting, and more. Your team's usage directly determines credit consumption. Unlike seat-only pricing, this consumption-based model scales with your adoption, not just headcount.

How is consumption-based pricing measured?
Consumption‑based pricing is measured based on discrete actions. An action is a specific AI‑powered task performed by the platform—such as generating a personalized message or completing an individual search—each defined with clear, measurable criteria and a predictable credit cost. Credits are then deducted according to the predefined amount associated with each action.
Can I increase the number of credits?
Yes. If your team needs more credits than what is included in your plan, you can purchase additional credit packs at any time.

What's the difference between Outreach's consumption-based pricing and seat-based pricing?
Outreach combines both models. Seat-based pricing gives each user access to the platform. Consumption-based AI credits let you pay for what you actually use—research, personalization, sequencing, forecasting, and coaching—so your costs scale with adoption, not headcount alone.

Your team's usage of these AI-powered features consumes credits.
Which Outreach tier is best for teams just getting started?
Amplify Essentials and Amplify Core are designed for teams getting started. Essentials (10K credits) focuses on conversation intelligence and coaching. Core (25K credits) emphasizes pipeline creation and account orchestration with AI agents. Both include foundational agents & AI, engagement, account planning, and conversation intelligence capabilities.
How does Outreach pricing compare to competitors like Gong or Salesloft?
Unlike single-point solutions, Outreach pricing covers the entire revenue lifecycle—prospecting, deal management, conversation intelligence, and forecasting—in one platform. You don't need to pay separately for each capability. AI credits scale with your adoption; you only pay for what you use.
