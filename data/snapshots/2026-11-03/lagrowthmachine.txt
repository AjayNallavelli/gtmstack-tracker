Lucky you! The free trial allows you to test

How many leads can I activate per month?
As many as you want.
La Growth Machine does not limit you in any way.

What are identities?
An identity is a seat for which you can
automate on all three channels
(LinkedIn + Email + Twitter).
Should you need to automate for 2 people, you will need 2 identities
How to add another identity?
You can add another identity (or seat) directly within the app.
Note:
During your free-trial you only have access to 1 identity, upgrade your account to add multiple identities.
What's the difference between Multichannel Inbox and Rotating Inbox?
The
Multichannel Inbox
is a
unified inbox
that brings together both your
LinkedIn and email conversations
in one place.
It helps you stay on top of all your multichannel interactions, get full visibility, and never miss a reply — no matter the channel your lead uses.
The
Rotating Inbox
, on the other hand, is all about
sending
.
It automatically distributes your outbound emails across multiple addresses to
preserve your domain’s deliverability
and
safely scale your outreach volume
.
📷 In short:
Multichannel Inbox = manage & read LinkedIn + email messages
Rotating Inbox = send smartly through multiple email addresses

What's the difference between an invited member (free) and an identity?
A member
is a person from your company who
has a La Growth Machine account and a widget
. A company can have as many members as specified in each plan (3 for the Basic plan and 25 for the Pro and Ultimate plans).
By inviting a
free member
, they will be able to:
Import leads, create leads, and prepare campaigns
Access the account to view campaign performance and qualify leads
Respond to LinkedIn messages and emails under each identity
A member cannot launch a campaign.
Their Google and LinkedIn accounts are not connected, so they cannot launch a campaign under their name. To do this, you need to have subscribed to an identity with La Growth Machine.
An identity equals a license.
An identity can then launch campaigns using their profile.
Depending on your plan,
it’s possible to have multiple identities:
Up to 3 identities with the Basic plan (3 licenses and therefore 3 billed identities)
No identity limit for the Pro and Ultimate plansClearer now? Come see for yourself!
