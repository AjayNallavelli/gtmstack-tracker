Frequently asked questions
How much does Smartlead cost?
+
Smartlead offers four plans: Base at $39/month, Pro at $94/month, Unlimited Smart at $174/month (most popular), and Unlimited Prime at $379/month. Annual billing saves 17% across all plans. All plans come with a free trial and no credit card is required to get started.
Do I have to pay per email account or mailbox?
+
No. All Smartlead plans include unlimited email accounts at no extra cost. You can connect as many mailboxes as you need and scale your outbound without paying per account, which is a significant cost advantage over most outbound tools.
What is the difference between a lead credit and an email credit?
+
A lead credit is a unique email address (prospect) stored in your account, counted toward your contacts limit. An email credit is each individual email sent across any campaign. For example, the Base plan includes 2,000 contacts and 6,000 email sends per month. These are separate counters.
How does email rotation work in Smartlead?
+
Smartlead automatically rotates sending across all connected mailboxes within a campaign. This distributes send volume evenly, protects individual sender reputation, and improves deliverability by avoiding high-volume spikes from a single address.
Does Smartlead require a long-term commitment?
+
No. Smartlead offers monthly and annual plans with no lock-in contracts. You can cancel anytime.
What is the pricing for whitelabeling, and which plan includes it?
+
Whitelabeling is available as an add-on at $29/month per client workspace. The whitelabelling lets agencies brand the platform as their own and manage each client from a separate workspace. Option to add clients is available from Pro Plan onwards and on the highest plan which is the Unlimited Prime - you already get 3 clients/workspaces added.
Does Smartlead include email verification?
+
Email verification is available as a value-added add-on across all plans. It helps improve deliverability by validating prospect emails before sending. Verification credits can be purchased on a one-time or monthly basis.
What types of businesses use Smartlead?
+
Smartlead is used by over 100,000 businesses, from solo founders and sales teams to lead generation agencies and Fortune 500 companies. The Base plan suits early-stage outreach; Pro fits growing sales teams; Unlimited Smart and Prime are built for agencies and high-volume enterprise outreach.
