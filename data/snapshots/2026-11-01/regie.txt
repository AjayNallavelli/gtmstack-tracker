Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a credit card to start?
No. Start free with 250 credits using just a work email. You only add a card if you upgrade to a paid plan.
Will AI-written email hurt my domain?
You send from your own connected Gmail or Outlook, every contact is verified before send so you're not hitting dead addresses, and sending is throttled with unsubscribes handled for you. Messages go out personalized and grounded, not mass-blasted, which is what protects your reply rates and sender reputation.
What happens when I run out of credits?
Research, drafting, enrichment, and dialing pause. On a paid plan they pick up again when your credits refresh, or straight away if the workspace owner has switched on overage billing, capped at a limit they set. On Free, upgrade to keep going. Extra credits can't be bought on their own. Your prospects, drafts, and settings stay exactly where they are.
Do credits roll over?
Paid plan credits don't. They reset at the start of each billing period, monthly or annual, rather than carrying over. The 250 credits each person starts with on Free are different: they never expire.
Do I have to switch off my current CRM?
No. RegieGO comes with its own built-in CRM, free on every plan, so you can start with nothing. If you use HubSpot, Salesforce, or Monday CRM, connect it on any plan and your records sync in, with a backfill of what is already there. On HubSpot and Salesforce, switch on write-back and RegieGO's work flows back too. Each workspace connects one CRM, and it runs alongside your existing tools instead of replacing them.
How is this different from just using ChatGPT?
ChatGPT and Claude can help you write, but standing up prospecting with them takes weeks of prompts and connectors, and you're the one keeping it running. RegieGO is built for the whole motion and comes pre-loaded: it builds your list, enriches and verifies contacts, drafts in your voice, and dials, so you're engaging on day one.
Can my team use RegieGO?
Yes. Free holds up to 10 people, and each person new to RegieGO brings 250 credits into one shared pool. Pro is for one person. For two or more people on a paid plan, Team is priced per seat with a shared credit pool, and the workspace owner adds seats in-app as you grow.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Pro and Team are self-serve with no contract. The workspace owner can cancel anytime from billing settings, and the plan stays active until the end of your current billing period, then moves to Free. An annual plan is paid for the year up front, so cancelling stops the renewal.
