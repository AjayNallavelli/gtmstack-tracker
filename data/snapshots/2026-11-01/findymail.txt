Quick answers about pricing
Findymail plans give you generous usage without counting credits. Here are the most common questions.
What are the usage limits on each plan, and what happens when I hit them?
Every plan comes with generous usage limits, so you can find and verify contacts without counting credits.
Everything you do in Findymail draws from the same pool, whether it's the web app, the Chrome extension, an integration or the API, and it's shared by everyone on your plan. How much you can do depends on what you run: finding a phone number, for example, uses more than finding an email.
Individual covers everyday prospecting. Team gives you 5× more usage than Individual, and Scale gives you 20×.
When you reach a limit, nothing is charged. You can wait for usage to free up or move to a higher plan. Need more than Scale?
Talk to us
about Enterprise.
What's a seat?
A seat is one team member with their own login. Individual includes 1 seat, Team 5 seats and Scale 20 seats. Need more? Enterprise plans come with custom seat counts.
What's an active signal?
An active signal is a signal you're tracking. It continuously monitors for intent signals from your ideal customers, like hiring, funding or job changes, and delivers matching leads to your feed. Each plan sets how many signals you can track at the same time.
Learn more about Signals →
Are these verified B2B emails?
Yes. Every email is verified in real-time when you request it. We don't pull from stale databases, but verify at the moment you need it, so you know it works right now.
Do I need to reverify Findymail data?
No. Our emails are already verified and ready to send.
Here's what happens if you run our data through a separate verification tool: they'll mark 15-20% of our emails as "invalid" or "risky" — even though they're real, working emails.
Why? Because we verify catch-all domains (emails competitors can't verify), and traditional verifiers flag them as risky. If you reverify our data, you're removing valid emails and paying twice for verification.
Our guarantee:
<5% bounce rate. Clay ranked us #1 with more valid emails than competitors. No need to waste money reverifying data that's already verified.

What's the difference between Finder credits and Verifier credits?
Finder credits
find and verify new contacts in one step. You give us a name and company, we give you a verified email. 1 email = 1 credit.
Verifier credits
are bonus credits to verify contacts from other sources (social profile exports, purchased lists, etc.). If you already have an email and just need to verify it, use a Verifier credit.
Findymail contacts are already verified, so you don't need to use Verifier credits on them.
Do I pay a credit if you can't find the email I need?
No. You only pay for verified results. If we can't find a working email, we don't charge you.

Yes. Unused credits roll over month-to-month, up to 2× your monthly allowance. So if you're on the 5,000-credit plan, you can bank up to 10,000 credits total.

What is Datacare?
Datacare is automated CRM enrichment & hygiene. We keep your CRM clean by continuously enriching and updating contact and company data, without you lifting a finger.
