Support
We are here to help you, just drop a line.
Is RocketReach free?
It is free to create a RocketReach account.
Free accounts have access to 3 free lookups with no credit card required.
You can then upgrade your account at any time to billing that is either monthly or annual.
Once you are signed up, feel free to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
What if I want to pay from my bank account?
Use PayPal to pay from your bank account. We accept most bank credit and debit cards.
Do you have larger plans? How do I get RocketReach for my team?
We can support plans and teams of any size. You can create a team from your
account page
. Please
contact us
if you have further questions.
Why is my credit card being declined?
The most common reason is your bank needs you to approve the transaction, so contact them. If declines persist, try the PayPal option. Please chat with us or email
support@rocketreach.co
for further assistance.
What happens if a lookup does not return verified email or phone?
A lookup is used if we find and verify email or phone, otherwise it is refunded.
Can I cancel my subscription when I want? Will I keep my lookups if I cancel?
Yes, you can cancel anytime through your accounts page. Since you prepay for a year or month, cancellation takes effect the following term. You retain access to your plan features for the full 1 year or 1 month term.
What if I just want professional or personal emails?
You can select to lookup only personal or only professional emails by updating your
email type preferences
. Once you make that selection, you will never see emails of the filtered type.
