Questions you might have
What are Lusha credits?
Lusha’s credit system is your way to access contact data, with more control and flexibility to meet your needs.
Credit-Based Pricing
• Reveal verified email address –
1
credit
• Reveal a phone number –
5
credits
With this structure, you only pay for what you need. It also allows you to prioritize what’s most valuable for your outreach efforts, whether it’s emails, phone numbers or a combination of both.
What is included in my Free plan?
The Free plan provides you with up to
40
credits
per month.
You’ll also have access to basic prospecting features as listed above, our browser extension
and CRM integrations to help you get started with Lusha.
How can I estimate my credit usage?
Your credits usage is based on the type of data you need for your outreach efforts:
•
Email Campaigns:
Multiply the number of prospects you plan to contact by
1
credit each.
•
Phone Outreach:
Allocate
5
credits per revealed number.
Need help planning?
Our team is here to assist you in assessing your needs and ensuring you have the right
amount of credits to achieve your goals.
Can I cancel my plan?
Yes, you can cancel your plan at any time!
Simply go to your ‘Account Settings’ and select the option to manage your subscription.
Should you choose to cancel, your credits will remain available until the end of your billing period.
All cancellations will take place at the end of your plan cycle.
How can I purchase credits or seats for my team?
You can add credits anytime through your account settings. If you need more seats or access to
additional features, simply upgrade to a higher plan tier.
Upgrades will take place immediately.
For teams larger than 5 users, please reach out to our Sales team.
Can I limit credit usage per user?
Yes, this feature is available in our Scale plans. We invite you to contact our sales team who will
be happy to assist you.
Will my plan renew automatically?
Yes, your plan renews automatically and all your monthly or annual credits are issued automatically.
What happens if I don’t use all our credits in a month?
On
monthly plans, as long as you’re subscribed, your unused credits will roll over and accumulate up to twice the credit limit according to your plan.
On annual plans, you will get all your credits up front. Any unused credits will be reset at the end of the annual cycle.
What if I need a custom plan?
Our scale plan is fully customizable and should fit most use cases. Talk to our sales reps and they’ll be happy to assist with understanding how many credits you need.
How can I check when my credits reset?
Your credits are tied to your billing cycle. When your current cycle ends, your credits will automatically reset. You can check your exact reset date anytime in Account Settings under the Billing & Subscription section.
