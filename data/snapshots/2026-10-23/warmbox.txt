Which plan should I choose?
The plan that we recommend will depend on your sending needs in terms of volume, and/or the number of email accounts you want to warm-up. If you are not sure, remember that you are free to upgrade your plan at any time!

Do you offer bigger plans?
Yes! We offer custom plans for Enterprises, Agencies & Corporates with bigger needs and substantial sending volumes. Don't hesitate to reach us on the chat to get more information.

Do you provide dedicated support?
Yes! All plans come with access will to our in-app chat support and online help center. Sales's subscribers can also be eligible for personalized onboarding and account manager.

How do I know when my inbox is warmed-up?
The time necessary for the warm-up depends of your email address, your email providers... As an indication, it's usually took between 15-30 days of warming without any interruption to see the first results.
