FAQ
All the answers you're looking for.
New to BetterContact? Here's everything you need to know about credits, verification, and how the waterfall works.
Can I try BetterContact for free?
Yes. We want you to see the power of BetterContact for yourself. That's why you get
50 credits free
when you sign up. No credit card needed.
What is a BetterContact credit?
Credits are how you find emails and phone numbers across 20+ data sources.
1 credit = 1 email + verification.
10 credits = 1 mobile phone + verification.
Credits are only charged when valid data is actually found.
What is waterfall enrichment?
On average, a single data source finds only 30–60% of emails or mobile numbers. Waterfall enrichment requests several sources one after another to find a person's contact details.
The more sources used, the more contact data found
, and the more potential customers you reach.
Does data verification cost extra?
No. Email verification through
Bouncer
for optimal deliverability is included in the credit cost. Real-time catch-all validation also doesn't incur any extra charge.
Do credits roll over?
Yes. We roll your unused credits over to the next month,
capped at twice your subscribed amount
. So on a 5,000-credit plan, you can hold a maximum of 10,000 credits.
Can I pay annually?
Yes. Switch to
annual billing
and you get
2 months free
: you pay for 10 months and keep all 12. Annual plans also extend credit roll-over to 12 months.
If an email or phone isn't found, am I charged?
No. There's
no charge
when an email or mobile number isn't found. A credit is only ever charged after successful verification of valid data.
