Affiliate disclosure

GTMStack Tracker is free to use. We plan to make money in three ways:

  • Affiliate links. Some "Visit" buttons may be affiliate links. They are labelled Affiliate and use rel="sponsored". If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Methodology

  1. Source. Each tool's official pricing page (linked on every tool page). We never estimate prices; if a vendor hides pricing behind "contact sales", we say so.
  2. Daily snapshot. A script fetches each pricing page (with a headless browser where the page needs JavaScript), extracts the visible text, keeps the lines that carry prices or billing terms, and stores a dated snapshot.
  3. Diff. Each snapshot is compared with the previous one. Changed price points become a "price change" entry; changed plan wording without price changes becomes a "plan / wording change". All entries appear on What changed.
  4. Structured plans. The plan tables are curated by hand from the page and re-checked when the diff flags a change.
  5. Caveats. "From $X/mo" is the lowest listed paid price and may be an annual-billing or per-seat price — see each table. Some vendors localise prices by visitor country; where we could only see a non-USD price we show it in the notes instead of converting.
